About Us

Home > About Us

Dreamtreats Cakes started as a dream in the heart of a 19 year old girl who first discovered her love for cakes and sugar-craft while watching her mother bake. 23 years later, Dreamtreats has grown to become a top class establishment specializing in creating exquisite couture cakes with superlative taste.

We have been in the business of crafting your dreams in sugar, creating our own unique taste and custom packaging such that from ordering, through design to delivery, we give our customers the specialty touch.

We are committed to making mouth watering cakes with exquisite designs using only the finest ingredients and innovative sugar-crafting techniques.

We have acquired for ourselves a reputation of delivering promptly and on time. We are committed to helping our customers make enduring memories and enhancing their relationships with their loved ones.

In response to growing trends and the challenges of the 21st century individual, Dreamtreats launched a full online store, Cakes @ Click. Just Click and we deliver, right at your doorstep.

It is simple as that. We also offer on-line baking classes and periodic newsletter delivered to your email when you sign up for it.

The creative mind behind Dreamtreats is Tinuola Okenimkpе. She studied Computer Science at the Yaba College of Technology. Under her directive, Dreamtreats has made spectacular cakes for celebrations and weddings.
